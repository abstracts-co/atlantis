We computed Principal Coordinates of Neighbour Matrices (PCNM) or classical distance-based Moran’s Eigenvector Maps (Borcard et al. 2011) based on their geographic locations. The first row shows PCNM1-PCNM5, the second row PCNM6-PCNM10, etc. Each plot shows the PCNM values according to their geographic location, with the full squares positive values, and open squares negative values. The size of the squares is proportional to the absolute value of the PCNM score (so a large open square corresponds to a large negative value, a large closed square to a large positive value, small squares to values close to zero). (TIF) Table S1 Means and SD of environmental variables measured in the sampled experimental (exp.) and reference (ref.) ponds throughout the study period. A MANOVA performed on all listed variables, using pond type as independent variables was significant (Wilks 0.559, F7,182= 20.49, p,0.001). Significant differences between ponds were only detected for DO (dissolved oxygen) concentration, DO saturation, and for pond size (Tukey’s HSD for unequal N posthoc test, p = 0.02, p = 0.04 and p,0.01, respectively).
