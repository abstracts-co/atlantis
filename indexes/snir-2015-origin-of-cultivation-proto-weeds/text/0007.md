These two types of food resources compensate for each other–birds are more available during autumn/winter while plants prevail in spring/summer. The circled numbers indicate the number of migratory bird species that currently visit the area only once a year. These were eaten from December to June at Ohalo II, and therefore serve as a good indicator of season of occupation of the site. These and other faunal remains point to year–round occupation of Ohalo II. (DOCX) S2 Fig. Darnel (Lolium temulentum). (A) Modern darnel and (B) archaeological darnel from Ohalo II.
